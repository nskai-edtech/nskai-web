The problem

A bank closes the day by hand. Statements arrive in four formats and the core ledger in a fifth, and the differences between them are found by eye.

Most lines pair without argument. The value sits in the handful that do not, and those are the ones the team runs out of time for.

Where it applies
Three places the same pairing engine is used.
01
The daily close

Statements against the core ledger, pairing first and exceptions after, so the close ends at a known list rather than a deadline.

02
Payments exceptions

Chargebacks, duplicates and failed settlements grouped by cause, with the rule that grouped them on the record.

03
Reporting packs

Regulatory and management figures assembled from source rows, each number traceable back to the entries behind it.
